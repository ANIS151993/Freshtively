# Freshtively

Freshtively is a homemade cultural food marketplace system. Its main goal is to connect people who want authentic homemade meals with local household cooks, then support delivery through a clear role-based workflow.

## What Is Freshtively?

Freshtively is designed for three main users:

- **Customers** who want to discover and order homemade cultural food.
- **Cookers** who want to sell trusted homemade meals from their own kitchens.
- **Delivery partners** who help move food from the cooker to the customer.

The system brings these roles together in one app so the full food journey is visible:

```text
Customer discovers food
  -> Customer places an order
  -> Cooker accepts and prepares the meal
  -> Delivery partner picks up the meal
  -> Customer receives and tracks the order
```

## Main Goal

The goal of Freshtively is to make homemade food easier to find, sell, and deliver.

Many cultural meals are made inside homes, not restaurants. Freshtively gives those home kitchens a digital marketplace where customers can find authentic food, cookers can manage their food business, and delivery partners can support local food movement.

## How Customers Use The App

Customers use Freshtively to find food that feels personal, local, and homemade.

Customer journey:

1. Open Freshtively.
2. Browse public pages to understand the platform.
3. Create a customer account.
4. Search or discover homemade dishes.
5. View dish details and cooker information.
6. Add food to the cart.
7. Review checkout information.
8. Place the order flow.
9. Track order status from preparation to delivery.
10. Manage profile, orders, notifications, support, and preferences.

Customer value:

- Discover homemade cultural food nearby.
- Order from local household cooks.
- Track the order process.
- Save time finding authentic meals.
- Support local home-based food businesses.

## How Cookers Use The App

Cookers use Freshtively to turn cooking skills and family recipes into a local food business.

Cooker journey:

1. Create a cooker account.
2. Build a cooker profile.
3. Complete kitchen and safety verification steps.
4. Add dishes with details, prices, and availability.
5. Receive order requests.
6. Accept or manage active orders.
7. Prepare the food.
8. Coordinate handoff with a delivery partner.
9. Track order history, reviews, earnings placeholders, and support.

Cooker value:

- Share cultural food with the local community.
- Manage dishes and orders from one dashboard.
- Build trust through profile and verification.
- Operate a flexible home kitchen business.
- Grow visibility beyond word-of-mouth.

## How Delivery Partners Use The App

Delivery partners use Freshtively to help homemade food reach customers safely and clearly.

Delivery journey:

1. Create a delivery partner account.
2. Complete profile, vehicle, and document steps.
3. View delivery requests.
4. Accept a delivery request.
5. Go to the cooker pickup location.
6. Confirm pickup.
7. Deliver the food to the customer.
8. Complete the delivery flow.
9. Track delivery history, ratings, earnings placeholders, and support.

Delivery partner value:

- Find local delivery opportunities.
- See clear pickup and drop-off flow.
- Support home-based food businesses.
- Help customers receive fresh homemade meals.

## Why This System Matters

Freshtively is built around trust, culture, and local connection.

It helps:

- Customers find food that may not be available in regular restaurants.
- Cookers share authentic homemade meals and earn from their skills.
- Delivery partners support a neighborhood-based food network.
- Communities keep cultural food traditions visible and accessible.
