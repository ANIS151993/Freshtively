Legal Agreements

1. Terms of Service for Home Cooks

By operating on the Freshtively platform, you acknowledge and agree that you are an independent contractor and not an employee of Freshtively. You are responsible for maintaining all necessary local permits and licenses required to operate a home kitchen in your jurisdiction.

2. Safety & Hygiene Standards

You agree to adhere to the Freshtively "Fresh-Kitchen" hygiene code, which includes mandatory handwashing protocols, temperature control for storage, and the exclusion of cross-contamination risks.

Freshtively reserves the right to perform unannounced kitchen inspections or request photo evidence of cooking environments to ensure quality and safety for our diners.

3. Liability Waiver

The Cooker agrees to indemnify and hold harmless Freshtively from any claims arising from foodborne illness, allergic reactions, or property damage resulting from the Cooker's operations.
