What is Freshtively?

Freshtively is a homemade cultural food marketplace system. It is built for communities where many meaningful meals are cooked at home, passed through family recipes, and shared through culture. The system gives those meals a digital place to be discovered, ordered, prepared, and delivered.

C

Customer side

Customers discover homemade dishes, review cooker information, add food to cart, and track the order.

K

Cooker side

Cookers create a profile, complete verification steps, publish dishes, and manage incoming orders.

D

Delivery side

Delivery partners accept requests, pick up meals, complete drop-offs, and support the final handoff.

Main goal of the system

The main goal is simple: make homemade food easier to find, sell, and deliver. Freshtively gives customers access to authentic meals, gives cookers a way to grow a home-based food business, and gives delivery partners a clear local delivery workflow.

Why it matters for customers

A customer may want food that tastes like home, but that food may not exist in regular restaurants. Freshtively helps the customer find cultural dishes from trusted local kitchens.

Why it matters for cookers

A cooker may have strong recipes and cooking skill, but no easy digital marketplace. Freshtively gives that cooker a profile, menu, and order workflow.

How each user uses the app

Freshtively is designed so every person sees the tools they need. Customers order, cookers prepare, and delivery partners move the food.

1

Customer

  1. Open Freshtively and create a customer account.
  2. Discover homemade cultural food.
  3. Choose a dish and review details.
  4. Add food to cart and review checkout.
  5. Track the order until delivery.

Value: Customers find authentic homemade meals and support local cooks.

2

Cooker

  1. Create a cooker account and profile.
  2. Complete kitchen and safety verification steps.
  3. Add dishes, prices, and availability.
  4. Accept orders and prepare meals.
  5. Coordinate handoff for delivery.

Value: Cookers can share cultural recipes and manage a flexible food business.

3

Delivery partner

  1. Create a delivery account.
  2. Complete profile, vehicle, and document steps.
  3. View and accept delivery requests.
  4. Pick up food from the cooker.
  5. Deliver the meal to the customer.

Value: Delivery partners help homemade food move through the neighborhood.

The full food journey

Step 4

Deliver

Step 5

Receive

The customer receives the food and can review the experience.

Why people can understand the idea quickly

It uses familiar roles

Everyone understands customer, cook, and delivery. Freshtively connects them in one system.

It protects local trust

The cooker profile and verification flow help customers feel more confident before ordering.

It keeps the process visible

The order journey is broken into clear stages, so each user knows what to do next.
